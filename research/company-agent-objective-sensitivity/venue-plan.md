# Venue and deadline plan

Verified 2026-07-23 from official venue pages. AoE deadlines convert to 20:59 KST
the following calendar day.

## Immediate route

1. **Primary: NeurIPS 2026 “Who Verifies the Agents?” workshop.**
   Deadline: 2026-08-29 23:59 AoE (2026-08-30 20:59 KST). Format: 4–9 pages
   excluding references/appendices, NeurIPS template, double blind, non-archival.
   Fit: agent verification, simulators, reward hacking, evaluation harnesses.
   Official: <https://verify-agents-workshop.github.io/>.
2. **Backup: Evaluation of Interactive Agents workshop.**
   Conference target deadline is Aug 29 AoE; full ≤9 pages, short ≤4, non-archival.
   The page is under construction and anonymity/artifact/final portal rules are not
   confirmed; recheck before submission.
   Official: <https://eval-interactive-agents-workshop.github.io/>.
3. **Policy-complete backup: FAST workshop.**
   Deadline Aug 29 AoE; full ≤7 pages or short ≤4, double blind, non-archival, at
   least one author attendance required.
   Official: <https://fast-workshop.github.io/>.

## Archival route

- **ICLR 2027 is the primary archival target**, but its CFP, deadline, page limit,
  anonymity, artifacts, and exact dates are not announced. Only “West Coast North
  America” is official. The ICLR 2026 dates/rules are planning proxies, not 2027
  facts. Monitor <https://iclr.cc/Conferences/FutureMeetings>.
- **AAAI-27 AI Alignment** is a relevant archival backup, but its separate
  schedule, portal, pages, anonymity, and artifact rules are TBA.
  Official: <https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/aaai-27/aia-call/>.

## Closed routes

- NeurIPS 2026 main and Evaluations & Datasets abstract/full deadlines were May 4/6
  AoE and are closed. Main/E&D use 9 content pages and double-blind review; E&D
  also requires accessible documented code/data at submission and public artifacts
  by camera-ready.
- AAAI-27 main required an abstract by Jul 21 AoE and is closed to new papers. A
  full paper can proceed only if an abstract was already registered.

## Submission positioning

The workshop paper should present the audit protocol, novelty audit, executable
synthetic pilot, CoffeeBench replication plan, and evaluator-validity threats.
It must not report heuristic simulations as frontier-model evidence. Ralphthon
ICML and Environment Foundry can motivate reproducibility and rights-aware
operations, but the paper must stand on scientific claims rather than marketing.
