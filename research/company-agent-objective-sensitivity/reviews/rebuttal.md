# Author response to the two independent red teams

## We agree

The broad novelty claim fails. KPI switching, objective-conditioned policies,
enterprise agents, and reward hacking all have direct precedents. We also agree
that the current synthetic pilot cannot support H1 or H2, that OCAD is a
closed-loop total contrast rather than a direct same-state effect, and that the
old bootstrap interval had no inferential meaning.

## Changes made in V0

- Reframed the contribution as a candidate empirical measurement package.
- Defined treatment as the complete objective-message package.
- Added first-decision and frozen shared-state direct diagnostics.
- Rewrote H1 to require repeat and paraphrase baselines plus vector effects.
- Rewrote H2 around KPI-matched benign policies or an estimated Pareto frontier.
- Replaced unsupported random-effects claims with clustered paired analysis and
  fixed case studies until enough levels exist.
- Removed the heuristic bootstrap interval and decomposed all 132 event records.
- Renamed the protocol a draft preregistration and added search, rights, and
  protocol-to-code receipts.

## Not fixed and therefore submission blockers

- No frontier or open-weight model has been run.
- No second environment has been licensed, implemented, or validated.
- Shared-state probes, paraphrase/intensity factors, blind graders, hidden
  stakeholder utility, and KPI-matched benign policies are planned but not coded.
- No public time-stamped preregistration exists.
- Literature coverage is non-exhaustive; priority remains provisional.

## Decision

Proceed only as a design/registered-report candidate. Do not submit the current V0
as an empirical benchmark paper. The first go/no-go experiment is whether the
effect survives paraphrases and appears in both direct shared-state probes and
closed-loop outcomes without relying on an authored deceptive policy.
